01

Who we are

IronForge Equipment Ltd (“IronForge”, “we”, “us”) supplies, installs and services commercial gym equipment. Our headquarters and showroom are at 100 Fitness Boulevard, Industrial Park, NY 10001.

This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

02

What this policy covers

This policy covers this website and the enquiries you send us through it. Equipment orders, installations and service contracts are covered by the written agreement you sign with us, which includes its own privacy terms.

03

Information you give us

The quote form on this website does not send anything to our servers. When you submit it, it opens your own email app with your answers written into a new message addressed to sales@ironforgefitness.com. We only receive that information if you choose to send the email.

A quote request usually includes:

  • your name, work email address and, if you add it, a phone number;
  • the type of equipment or service you need;
  • details about your facility, such as floor size and opening date.

We also receive whatever you tell us when you call us, email us directly or visit the showroom.

04

Information collected automatically

The company that hosts this website may keep standard server logs, which record your IP address, browser type, the pages you requested and the time of each request. These logs are used to keep the site secure and running.

Some parts of the page are loaded from third-party services, which receive your IP address when your browser requests them:

  • Unsplash. Photographs on this site are loaded from the Unsplash image service (images.unsplash.com). Your browser requests each image directly, so Unsplash receives your IP address and standard request information.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare). The animation library used on the home page is loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, which receives your IP address and standard request information when your browser downloads it.
05

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not set cookies. It stores one small preference in your browser's local storage, under the key ironforge-theme, so the colour theme you choose is still selected on your next visit. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

06

How we use your information

We use the information you send us to:

  • prepare the layout and line-item price you asked for, and reply to your request;
  • arrange showroom visits, site surveys, installations and service visits;
  • keep records of quotes and orders;
  • meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not use your details for unrelated marketing unless you ask us to keep you informed.

07

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We share it only when it is needed to do the work you asked for, for example with a delivery or installation subcontractor, and with providers who run our email and business systems. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

08

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests for as long as we need them to prepare and follow up on the quote. If you become a customer, we keep order and service records for as long as the equipment is under warranty or service with us, and for any longer period our record-keeping obligations require.

09

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you live, including California, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honour them.

To make a request, email sales@ironforgefitness.com. We will reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

10

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold. No system is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information, such as payment card details, by email.

11

Children

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted on this page when they take effect.
