How a fit-out runs

3 phases, one crew, and no handoff to a subcontractor you have never met.

Plan the floor
We measure the space, map how members move through it, and return a layout with load points, clearances and power runs marked. You approve the floor before anything ships.
Install and commission
Our own crews deliver, assemble and test every station, then walk the floor with you and hand over a signed commissioning sheet for each machine.
Service it for life
Scheduled maintenance visits, cable and upholstery replacement, and a parts inventory held in stock so a repair does not become a 6-week wait.

Test the kit before you buy it

Our 20,000 sq ft headquarters holds a working showroom. Load a bar, run the belt, and sit on every machine on your list before you commit.
